Running your own website

Everything on the site — the beer list, the hours, what's on, the red banner across the top — is yours to change, from any phone or laptop, without asking anyone.

Getting in

There's no "log in" button on the site, on purpose. The way in is a small Owners link in the very bottom-right corner of any page.

  1. 01

    Scroll to the bottom of any page and tap Owners.

  2. 02

    Enter the password. It's the same one for both of you — keep it in your password manager rather than a note or a text message.

  3. 03

    You'll land on Manage the site. You stay signed in for about a month, so you won't be typing that password often.

Bookmark it once you're in. Nothing links to this page from anywhere else, and search engines are told to ignore it.

What each tab changes

Six tabs across the top. Each one edits a different part of the site.

Drink Menu

Every beer, cocktail and seltzer — name, style and ABV. You can rename a section, add items, remove them, or add a whole new section.

Food & Drink, and the On Tap list on the home page
Food Truck

Which truck is out front, when it's there, and what they're serving.

the panel at the bottom of Food & Drink
Hours

Your opening hours. Both halves are free text — write Tue–Thu, Sun and 12pm – 10pm, or Closed for a private event. Nothing is fussy about the format.

the footer of every page, plus Find Us and Contact
Events

Anything happening in the taproom. The date is free text too, so September 12, 2026 and Every Thursday both work.

What's On, and the red block on the home page
Banner

The red strip across the very top of the site. Untick the box to hide it — the preview underneath shows exactly how it'll look.

the top of every page
Messages

Anything sent through the contact form. Reply by email opens your mail app with their address already filled in.

nowhere public — these are only for you

Saving

There's no Save button. Type, and it saves itself a moment later. The word next to Sign out tells you where things stand.

Unsaved…

You've just typed something. It saves a half-second after you stop.

Saving…

On its way.

Saved

Done, and already live on the site. This clears after a second or two.

Not saved

Something went wrong — usually a dropped signal. Your typing is still on screen and hasn't been lost. Tap Retry. If it keeps failing, leave the tab open and try again when you have a better connection.

Changes are live the moment they save. Refresh the public page if you want to see it.

Two things you can't undo

Both ask first — read the question before tapping through

  • Reset to defaults throws away everything you've typed across every tab and puts back the original wording the site launched with. It is not an "undo my last change" button.
  • Delete on a message removes it for good. If there's any chance you'll want it later, reply by email first — that keeps a copy in your inbox.

Worth doing soon

Rotating Taps, Cocktails, Seltzer and Non-Alcoholic are all empty, as is the food truck list. Until something's added, each shows just its heading on the menu page.

If something looks wrong

Try the ordinary things first: refresh the page, and check you're still signed in. If you've been away a while the session may have lapsed — sign in again and your work will still be in the box where you left it.
